Guidance means resolving a problem or **difficulty** (**dificules**) given by someone in authority. **Guidance means help or advice to deal with a problem** or difficult situation.

For al-Ghazali, guidance (hidayah) is a two-part process of both outward _religious discipline_ and inward spiritual purification, with the ultimate goal being piety (taqwa) and closeness to God.
The "beginning of guidance" involves the proper performance of worship, avoiding sins, and following _etiquette with people_.
while at the "end of guidance" is the internal, spiritual state of a pure heart, free from negative traits like arrogance and filled with positive ones like _sincerity and humility_.
