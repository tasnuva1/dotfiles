First of all the share of financial assets and the total assets they have increased quite dramatically. Traditionally, all companies might own some financial assets for various reasons, but most of the things they own were like machines, buildings and so on.

--

In the US the share of financial assets and the total assets of known financial companies was just over one quarter in the 1970s. By the mid 2000s this has *risen to around half. and many loan financial corportions (GE Capital, Ford Finance) actually created financial subsidiaries, basically moved into financial business. These companies had made more profits than the original company. *Between 2001 and 2003 Ford made all its profit in Ford finance.* And suppose we are a car company, but a car business that doesn't make money.
